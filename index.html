BEFORE THE START

Good to know.

A few answers before you head to the paddock.

Is Turf Legends multiplayer?

It is a single-player game. You race against computer-controlled rivals with different racing styles.

Can I practice without using energy?

Yes. Free practice lets you experiment without spending career energy. Practice does not award career rewards.

Which languages are included?

English and German. The game follows your device language where supported, and you can change it manually.

Is there real-money betting?

No. The racing and progression systems do not offer real-money betting or cash prizes.

Where can I download it?

The Google Play release is being prepared. A verified download link will appear here when it is available.
