Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

1. Scope and controller

This policy covers this website and the pre-release Turf Legends configuration described below. The controller for its own processing is Manfred Uschan (OnvexaLabs), Unterneuberg 136, 8225 Pöllau, Austria. Further contact details are in the legal notice. Privacy contact: onvexalabs@gmail.com.

2. Visiting this website

This static website uses no analytics, advertising scripts, tracking pixels, embedded videos, external fonts, accounts or contact forms. Its code sets no cookies and writes no local or session storage. Images, scripts and styles are served with the site.

GitHub Pages hosts the website. GitHub states that it logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security, whether or not a visitor is signed in. Requests also necessarily transmit technical connection information. GitHub explains its processing, retention and international transfers in its privacy statement. Hosting is intended to serve the site reliably and protect it from misuse; the basis for the publisher’s necessary hosting processing is legitimate interest in secure delivery under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

3. Email support

If you email us, your address, message, attachments and information you choose to include are processed to answer your request. Please do not send passwords, signing keys, payment information or unnecessary personal data. Email is handled through the supplied Gmail mailbox; Google and your own mail provider process messages under their applicable terms. See Google’s privacy policy.

Depending on your request, the basis is taking steps at your request or providing support under Article 6(1)(b), or our legitimate interest in responding under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Resolved support correspondence is deleted after 12 months unless a legal retention obligation or ongoing dispute requires longer storage. Open requests are retained until resolved; the 12-month period then begins.

4. Game saves and device settings

The Android game is pre-release. The following information describes the reviewed development configuration. These details will be reconciled with the actual distributed build before its store release.

The reviewed game stores career progress, horses, settings and related gameplay state locally on the device. No studio-hosted account or cloud-save service is implemented in the reviewed source. Device or operating-system backup settings can affect copies of app data. The publisher must verify those settings in the final Android package.

You can remove local game data through Android’s app storage settings. This permanently removes local progress; backups, if any, are managed separately through your device or platform. Uninstall behavior depends on the platform and its backup settings. We cannot restore a local save that we do not hold.

5. Advertising and release verification

The reviewed production configuration has rewarded ads disabled. An AdMob integration is present in the project. Disabling a reward button does not establish that every bundled SDK is inactive. Before release, the publisher must verify the signed build’s network behavior, permissions, SDK versions and Google Play Data safety answers.

If a later release enables ads, its policy must explain the actual data flows, purposes, recipients, legal bases and consent choices before distribution. Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK can collect IP addresses, interactions, diagnostics and device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google’s SDK disclosure. No live ad-reward availability is promised on this website.

6. Younger players

The website has no registration or user submissions. The planned adult-only ad-bonus option does not make the whole game adults-only. Before release, the publisher must confirm the actual intended age groups, store declarations and any requirements for children’s data. Do not send a child’s personal information in a support request unless necessary to handle that request.

7. Your rights

Where the GDPR applies and its conditions are met, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction and data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the basis. You may complain to a competent data-protection authority, including in your place of residence, work or an alleged infringement. In Austria, you can contact the Austrian Data Protection Authority. Contact the privacy email above; sufficient verification may be needed to protect your data.

8. Changes and external links

External links load third-party sites only when followed. Their privacy notices apply there. This policy must be updated when the website or game’s data practices change. The date above identifies the current version.

9. Recipients, transfers and required information

Recipients, as necessary, include the website host GitHub, email provider Google and legally competent authorities. GitHub and Google may process data outside the EEA. Their linked privacy notices explain the safeguards they use, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where applicable. GitHub determines retention for hosting security logs; we do not set a separate retention period for those logs.

You do not have to send us a support message. Without a reply address and a description, however, we may be unable to address your request. This website does not perform profiling or automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. We do not sell support correspondence.
