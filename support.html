Contact the studio

Send questions, bug reports and feedback to onvexalabs@gmail.com. For a bug, include your device model, Android version, game version and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help; hide personal information first.

Lost progress

Career progress is stored locally. Clearing app data can permanently remove it, and we cannot restore saves we do not hold. Before reinstalling to fix a problem, contact us and describe what happened.

Practice, pause and language

Use free practice to learn without spending career energy. Pause an active race while the app remains open; closing it fully forfeits an unfinished race. English and German can be selected in the game.

Privacy and deletion requests

Use the same email address for privacy requests. Tell us which support correspondence your request concerns without sending sensitive identity documents unless specifically needed. No website account exists to delete. For local saves, see the privacy policy.
